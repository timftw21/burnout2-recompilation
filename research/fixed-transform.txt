NV097 fixed transform contracts

Fixed and programmable transforms share a 192-vector context. Composite rows
occupy 0..3, projection 4..7. Four model-view/inverse pairs occupy 8..39, eight
vectors per pair. Eye position is 56, fog plane 57, viewport scale/offset 58/59.
Each texture stage uses eight vectors from 64: texgen planes first, matrix second.
Method aliases now update that same context rather than unrelated register data.

Skin modes 0..6 select no blending, 2/3/4 matrices with a generated final weight,
or 2/3/4 explicit weights. Normals use inverse model-view rows, with optional
normalization. The unskinned composite already includes model-view. The skinned
composite transforms the blended eye position. Texture generation precedes its
texture matrix. Fog comes from specular alpha, radial distance, signed/absolute
plane distance or the fog vertex attribute; the final combiner consumes it.

The shared offscreen suite passes 133 checks on RTX 5060 Ti and WARP with no D3D11
validation messages. New cases cover all skin modes, normal transforms, texgen,
texture matrices, fog sources and raw GPU packet aliases. Fixed lighting and other
dependent texture modes remain incomplete; component checks do not prove parity.
