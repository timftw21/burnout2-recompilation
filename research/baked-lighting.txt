Baked lighting / ambient occlusion investigation (2026-10-16)

Finding: original assets contain stored texture shading and per-vertex shading.
A specifically ambient-occlusion bake is not established. Do not describe this
investigation as proof that Burnout 2 either uses or excludes baked AO.

Source: supported US Xbox XISO in Burnout 2/. Verified executable SHA-256:
B5207C40C11D7C5B5E129EF555F7632FA3584A4240BD0421C2B1B9AD9BFED7BF,
matching knowledge/supported_target.json. Original files were read only.

Bounded original-asset inspection:
- Inventoried 2,833 named texture records (896 distinct names) in all 15 forward
  track PSS packages. This was a metadata inventory, not visual review of every
  texture. No texture name identified AO, ambient occlusion or a lightmap.
  Naming alone cannot establish whether AO was included in ordinary textures.
- Each package has B2300008 at +0, texture count at +10, and pointer table at
  +1C0 (+14 contains 8381C1C0). Pointers use image base 8381C000. Checked native
  records contain the name at +10, raster at +60, dimensions at +6C/+70,
  compression byte at +A5, mip count at +A8 and pixel pointer at +B4.
- Matched all bytes of 106 captured texture payloads to their original dstl.pss
  payloads, using the recorded address minus 0381C000 as the file offset.
  The existing capture was frame-562156488000; no new gameplay was run.
- Decoded 16 original architecture textures from aptl.pss and dstl.pss using
  native DXT rendering. APT_Boarding_Wall has soft dark shading under its ledge;
  APT_Window_Cargo has shading around/below its window. DST_Steps has shaded
  stair surfaces and DST_WallConc2 is darker near its top edge. These details
  already exist in the texture pixels, independently of runtime world lighting.
  Paint, photographic source imagery, direct-shadow baking and AO baking can
  produce similar details. Their appearance does not identify the authoring method.

Original mesh/shader verification:
- Read only seven known meshes in dstl.psw section 19: 3,4,8,11,12,13,14.
  Their vertex prefixes match captured draws 408..414 exactly. Each uses the
  captured shader 2 and a 24-byte vertex: position +0, UV +12, packed BGRA +20.
- Original shader records at XBE VA 0033E160 and 0033F3F0 both match all ten
  captured instructions exactly. Slot 6 copies vertex attribute 3 RGB directly
  to output diffuse RGB; slot 7 supplies alpha from constant 100.w. These
  sampled meshes do not calculate their diffuse shading from normals at runtime.
- The original stored RGB varies substantially within meshes. Section 19 mesh
  12 (DST_GrassGreen) has 321 vertices and 101 distinct RGB values, with mean
  RGB brightness from 8 to 171 out of 255. Mesh 4 (DST_WallConc3) has 116 vertices,
  41 RGB values and brightness from 9 to 143. This verifies stored vertex shading;
  the RGB field does not say how the artist or exporter generated those values.

Limits: vehicles and reverse/crash-specific packages were not audited. No
original art source, exporter setting or developer statement identifying an AO
bake was found in the inspected material. AO could have been merged into the
stored RGB or ordinary texture pixels, leaving no separate AO map or flag.

Private reproducible inputs, scripts, CSV inventories and structured results:
build/checks/baked-ao-20261016/. original-textures.png is the labeled contact
sheet; report.json contains counts, shader provenance and sampled mesh ranges.
The private render helper links the existing native shader bank. No game code,
settings, saves or source assets were changed; no benchmark or computer use ran.
