Static callback discovery beyond direct calls

Frontend global 31FC88 points to preinitialized object 31FC98, whose first field
is method table 2B4CC0. 2A5C0 copies the object into its entries; 2A741 invokes
the first method, 309A0. Discovery follows at most three file-backed data pointers
from literals or loaded globals, validates a code-pointer method group, and
compiles its contiguous methods. Mutable dispatch remains unresolved in reports.

F6D70 is the RenderWare stream registrar. Argument 1 is a plugin registry and
argument 2 is an identifier. It stores arguments 3/4/5 at plugin fields
0Ch/10h/14h. F6DB0 loads the read callback at F6E98 and invokes it at F6EAD.
FEC90 forwards identifier/read/write/size plus registry 344BD8 to F6D70.
102240 registers 101DB0/101D90/101DE0 for plugin 50Eh through that wrapper.
Discovery recognizes the complete forwarding sequence and follows its callback
arguments across all matching object-type wrappers, rather than seeding the
single callback encountered during boot.

The material-effects atomic constructor 1018B0 saves the original atomic object
method (+10h) in its plugin record (+4) and installs wrapper 101870 at +10h.
101870 reads that saved method and calls it before updating material effects.
Discovery validates the complete constructor before following the method write.

EF9C0 attaches frame-linked objects; their +10h callback must also be compiled.
FDC60 initializes an atomic with FDB80 at +10h and FD250 at +48h. The latter is
loaded into EAX before being stored. Discovery follows literal/register method
writes for objects passed to the verified frame setter, including the atomic's
second method. FDB80 updates flags and, when required, calls FD450.

UI draw callback family

1EF10 initializes a position/colour value object with BFBF0 at +48h (1EF2E).
1E720 installs BFE70 into the same field (1E749). Inlined constructors repeat
these writes, and 32770 also replaces that field with BFD30 (3292E).
The original queued draw consumer C1210 uses a linked record: position starts
at +8h, colour at +18h and callback at +50h. C1306 calls that callback with the
calculated position/colour and record data; C1309 follows the next link.
Static discovery validates constructor and consumer bytes and includes literal
code pointers installed at +48h in functions calling or inlining those verified
UI constructors. An offset alone does not identify an object type. Mutable
dispatch remains unresolved rather than being forced to one target.
C14A0 obtains a pooled queue record through D9DA0 and returns its +8h value
object; it also sets that object's +40h/+44h ownership fields. Its callers can
install the same +48h draw method. For example, 31F30 writes 31E60 at 32159.
Discovery verifies the complete factory before including its callers.
Text constructors use the same layout without calling a generic initializer:
229D0 installs C0080 (229F9), 2EF40 installs C0390 (2EF64), 303B0 installs
C04A0 (304B0), and C0E70 installs C0890 (C0F7D). C1020 and C1070 call C0E70,
then replace its method with C08F0 and C0A60 at C1057 and C10A7 respectively.
Their original initializers, stores and shared consumer establish the whole
text callback family; discovery includes callers and inlined installations.

Loaded globals must pass through an initialized object before their method table
is considered. The CRT character table referenced at 1205DC through 347ED0 holds
packed 16-bit classification flags such as 00100010, not function pointers.

File-worker completion interfaces

1107C0 submits an open job with callback argument 3; 110870 submits a read job
with callback argument 4; 1108C0 submits a seek job with callback argument 3.
These are one-based argument numbers. Each stores the method at job +20h and
context at +24h. 1109B0 calls it at 110A64 (and 110AF8 for failed opens) with
{handle, byte count, error, context}. All three producers call enqueue 110690.
117260 takes a completion method as argument 6, writes it at context +4 and
submits wrapper 117240 through 1107C0. The wrapper rewrites handle/context
arguments and tail-calls that stored method at 117258. Discovery follows these
verified producer interfaces for the whole job family.

Media stream descriptors

11D730 copies descriptor +3Ch/+40h to stream +D8h/+DCh. 11CA40 invokes that
completion at 11D2E2. The descriptor at 116740 is a local 68-byte record with
116680 at +3Ch. Discovery follows the second argument's stack address and that
field's initializer across the verified cdecl helpers 11B430 and 11B730.
116680 also initializes a {116270, stream context} notification tuple before
binding its queue through 11B730. Queue producers' paired method/context writes
are additional compilation roots; their original dispatch remains intact.
111710 installs the manager observer {110C60, self} at +28h/+2Ch (1117AA).
11B430 binds its pointer from descriptor +0Ch to state +2Ch; 11B4B0 invokes
{method, context} at 11B4F1. Discovery also validates this constructor's tuple.

Pool allocator forwarding wrappers

106E90 and 108C60 prepend their pool object to five arguments and call 109EC0.
Their fourth incoming argument becomes the allocator's fifth argument: method.
1151A0 passes 1150F0 through 106E90 at 11541A. Discovery recognizes the complete
five-argument forwarding sequence, call target and cdecl cleanup across this
wrapper family rather than adding that one callback as a manual seed.

110C90 iterates the manager's linked sources and invokes argument 2 at 110CE2
with {source, context}. 110D10 walks a source chain and invokes the same argument
at 110D25. 11106E/111081 pass 111040 through these interfaces. Both verified
iterator ABIs are included in callback-argument discovery.

11B620 initializes a three-word {object, method, context} source-action record
with D87E0 at +4h. 11B650 invokes that field at 11B65B. Specialized initializers
such as 116510 replace it: 116526 installs 1162C0 and 116533 stores its context.
Discovery follows +4h method installations for this verified initializer family.

109DE0 forwards resource-release callback argument 2 and context argument 3
through the descriptor's +34h release method and then through 10D0C0. The latter
invokes that callback at 10D10D with {resource, context}. 115D30 passes 115140
through this interface at 115D8B. Both verified producers are discovery roots
for release completions, complementing the existing allocation interface.

111E00 prepares a source-close descriptor with context at +28h (111E0A) and
1115F0 at +24h (111E0D). The source constructor copies the pair into source
+27Ch/+280h; 1155C0 invokes it at 115A26 on close. Verified tuple discovery
accepts both adjacent write orders, since context can precede the method.

CRT floating conversion initializer 11E251 loads EAX with 12186B, then stores it
in global slots 347EB4 (11E256) and 347EC8 (11E283). The formatter calls 347EB4
at 121EAA. Global callback-store discovery follows bounded, uninterrupted MOV
register chains as well as immediate writes. The batch still requires an actual
call through the destination slot before scheduling its stored code pointer.

Long argument preparation in geometry queries

90420 invokes incoming argument 3 at 9059E (CALL [ESP+1A4h]); its verified
consumer ABI was already registered. B2530 pushes B22A0 at B2792, then performs
vector calculations and writes unrelated local fields before pushing its other
two arguments and calling 90420 at B283B. The old 32-instruction local scan
stopped before that legitimate callback push. Verified consumer argument scans
now allow 128 instructions, retaining their stack-rewrite and control-flow
barriers. B22A0 is discovered from the original argument setup, without a manual
seed, runtime decoder or substitute collision result.

Indexed stream completion arrays

116E70 initializes its stream through 11B430 and allocates separate request,
notification and context arrays. At 116F34 it loads the notification array from
owner +74h; 116F37 writes 116D80 to array + index*8. It reloads that same array
at 116F41, adjusts the context array pointer by the same byte offset, and stores
the context at notification +4h (116F46). Each request receives a pointer to its
notification pair at +10h (116F52). 1155C0 invokes that pair at 11584B with
{request, context}. 116D80 updates the outstanding-request mask and invokes the
parent completion only when its remaining requests have finished.

Discovery recognizes the complete indexed method/context setup in verified
stream constructors. The array reload, shared offset, separate context origin
and +4h context store must all agree; arbitrary code-looking integers are not
callbacks. This extends installation discovery without manually seeding the
missing method or claiming that mutable indirect dispatch is closed.

Selected UI methods and mixed stack arguments

1D180 reads incoming argument 8 from ESP+20h and installs it at UI object +48h
(1D184/1D190), then returns with 20h stack cleanup. 26740 selects 24440 or 25150
into EDI in switch cases at 26813/2682D/26847/26861. The branches merge at 26870;
the verified C14A0 queue factory preserves EDI. 268AB pushes that register, then
the caller reserves two float argument slots at 268B1 before calling 1D180 at
268D2. C1210 invokes the installed method at C1306.

Callback discovery follows verified stack slots through both pushes and explicit
argument reservations. Register origins follow the recovered control-flow graph,
including all switch branches; only the verified queue factory can be crossed
when tracing its preserved registers. Unknown writes and other calls remain
barriers. The walk is bounded to 4096 states within one recovered function; it
does not decode other functions or retain the executable instruction set.

Collision dispatch matrix

6A6A0 computes row*4+column from its object-kind arguments and loads the method
from 336E48 at 6A6AD. Two conditional paths reorder the objects before CALL EAX
at 6A6E6. The original 64-byte matrix contains seven unique methods: 6AAA0,
6A210, 6A550, 6A5C0, 6A420, 6A440 and 6A630. Null entries are deliberate; the
next field at 336E88 begins the non-code string '.rtd'. A driving collision
reached 6A210 before this matrix was discovered.

Discovery now follows actual register origins for indirect calls too. A called
32-bit indexed load from a file-backed code-pointer group identifies the dispatch
array; null entries are permitted, while a nonzero non-code entry terminates the
group. The same branch-aware walk finds the whole collision matrix and other
matching original dispatch groups, without a seed for the encountered method.

Complete CRT conversion interface

11E251 initializes the six adjacent callback cells 347EB4..347EC8. Its complete
56-byte installer matches the preserved PHASE7_RUNTIME_CALLBACK_INSTALLER_BYTES
and the original executable. Discovery retains the whole verified interface,
including 121561 and 12157B, whose cells have no currently observed call. The
individual absolute stores still remain available for cross-function call proofs.
